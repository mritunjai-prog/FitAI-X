Meal & Nutrition Planner — Redesigned

Same system as Workout Hub: zero containers, mesh gradient base, hairline hierarchy, gold accent. Four tabs — Today, Plan, Grocery, Insights. Everything is live: tap glasses to log water, check off groceries, switch days, expand the AI coach, swipe actions on meals.

What changed vs. your current NutritionScreen

  • Every card removed — macroCard, mealCard, groceryItem and both modal sheets lose their borderRadius + borderWidth + backgroundColor surfaces. Content sits directly on the mesh.
  • Modals → tabs. Grocery was a bottom sheet you had to open; it's now a first-class tab with a budget rail. Version history moves inline under each meal instead of a second sheet.
  • Calorie hero — 44px mono remaining-count beside a consumed/target ring, replacing the small "2050 kcal" label.
  • Macros — full-width rows with a goal tick-mark on each track and grams-left underneath, instead of three cramped columns.
  • Hydration — 8 tappable glass segments (tap to fill/empty) rather than two +250/+500 buttons; the buttons remain as a secondary affordance.
  • Meals — a real time-anchored timeline with a continuous rail, logged/now/pending nodes, inline P·C·F chips and cost. Swipe-to-regenerate becomes visible text actions, because a hidden swipe is undiscoverable.
  • Plan tab — 7-day strip with per-day kcal, constraint chips, AI composer, and per-meal edit rows.
  • Grocery tab — budget rail at top, category sections, check-off rows with strikethrough, running total in the dock.
  • Insights tab — 7-day calorie bars with over-target in red, stacked macro split, adherence and cost trend.
  • Both themes — macro colours retuned for light mode (#4ADE80 → #4E7C0A etc.) so they pass contrast on a light background.
